Your timetable and school notices

Find My Block helps students plan routes around Burnside High School. Google sign-in and account syncing have been removed. The site does not request your Google credentials or access to Google Calendar, Drive or contacts.

Imported .ics timetables, room mappings and manual map choices stay in your browser. They are not sent to the server. Remove timetable clears the active local timetable. Previously saved account records are not accessed by this version and have not been erased.

School notices are read from Burnside High School’s public notices page. Public notice text may be cached to keep the map available; cached notices show their date and freshness. No device location is collected.

School map and notice content: Burnside High School. Find My Block is a student project.
