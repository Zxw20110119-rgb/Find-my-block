How to play

  1. Tap a letter on the map or choose a block from the menu. Pick a tower to build it. Hover or focus a tower option to preview its range.
  2. Launch a wave. Towers fire automatically. The dashed lines show the paths bots will take, and each bot that reaches the Library costs health.
  3. Spend earned credits to expand and upgrade. Protect the Library through all eight waves.
