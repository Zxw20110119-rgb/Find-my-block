Your timetable and account

Find My Block helps students plan routes around Burnside High School. Using the map does not require an account.

If you choose Google sign-in, the site receives your Google account identifier, email address and display name. It uses the account identifier to keep your saved timetable separate from other accounts. It does not request access to Google Calendar, Google Drive or your contacts.

Imported calendar events and room-to-block settings are stored in the site's cloud database when you save them while signed in. The timetable is loaded when you return to the same account. A temporary device copy may be kept while syncing. Guests can keep a timetable in their browser without uploading it.

The GitHub Pages version opens a sign-in window on the main Find My Block site and uses the same account database. A connection token lasts up to one hour and is kept in the current tab's session storage, not local storage. You may need to reconnect after closing the tab or when the connection expires; your cloud timetable stays saved. Signing out of GitHub Pages disconnects that tab. Sign out of the main site separately if you also used it on a shared device.

To remove your saved lessons, use Remove timetable while signed in and wait for saving to complete. Sign out on a shared device. Your Google password is handled by Google and is never sent to this site. This site does not use device location.
